# AIOS Hero — NOTES.md

## What this section is
The first screen of the AIOS page. The visitor has already self-identified as a business owner (they clicked "AIOS for Business"), so this hero's job is NOT routing — it is **conviction**. In one screen it must say what AIOS is in concrete terms, who it is for, prove it is a real working thing, and offer one action: book a call.

## Job it must do
Make an SME founder think "this is for me, and it actually works" in 5 seconds — then give them the button. Conviction + proof + one CTA. Nothing decorative competes with that.

---

## Layout (structure)

**Split-screen hero — copy left, live product visual right.** Roughly 45% copy / 55% product on desktop (Vercel/Linear register). This is the AIOS equivalent of the Home hero's split, but the right side is a **real product mockup**, not a shader.

Left column (copy stack, left-aligned):
- Eyebrow chip
- Headline (two-line, outcome-led)
- Sub-line (names audience + mechanism)
- Single primary CTA button (`.btn-primary`)
- Micro-proof line directly under the CTA (`--text-small`, `--fg-3`)

Right column (proof visual) — **"One message → finished artifact":**
- A short trigger at the top: one incoming team message in a small WhatsApp/Telegram bubble (e.g. "Draft the offer letter for our new hire.")
- The hero of the visual is the **finished document it produced**, rendered large and real — the actual generated PDF (offer letter or invoice) sitting in a clean frame, legible enough to read a line or two. The proof is "look at the finished thing it made," NOT "look, it chats."
- Deliberately distinct from the Home AIOS-teaser, which shows the chat thread + a file *chip*. Here the artifact itself is the centrepiece. Same product world, fresh framing — a visitor arriving from Home does not see a rerun.
- Sits on `--bg`; AIOS register = tighter/crisper shadow (`--shadow-card`, not a soft glow). The document frame may carry the slightly stronger `--shadow-card` to lift it off the page as the focal object.

On mobile: stack vertically. Copy first, then the artifact below at ~90% width, centred (trigger bubble small above it). Single CTA stays above the fold — do not push it below the artifact on mobile.

Section background: `--bg` (warm off-white, page default). No dark hero.

**Single CTA only.** The BLUEPRINT's secondary "Watch the 90-second walkthrough" is **dropped** — the video does not exist and a dead button on the money page is worse than no button. If a real walkthrough ships later, add it then as a ghost secondary.

## The proof visual (detail for Claude Code)

Concept: **"One message → finished artifact."** A small trigger message produces a real, readable document, and the document — not the chat — is the centrepiece.

- **Trigger** (small, top): one team-member bubble with a plain work request, e.g. "Draft the offer letter for our new hire." Keep it to one bubble; this is just the spark.
- **Artifact** (large, focal): the generated document rendered as a real page in a clean frame — an offer letter or invoice with a letterhead, a few legible lines, and a subtle "generated by AIOS" / filename marker. It must look like a finished deliverable, not a placeholder rectangle.
- Motion: minimal. On scroll-into-view the document may fade/scale in once (~0.6s, single play, no loop) as if it just landed. AIOS register is snappy, not playful — no continuous animation.

Why this over the Home approach: Home shows the *chat thread + file chip*; this hero shows the *finished thing*. Same product, distinctly different artifact, so a visitor coming from Home doesn't see a rerun — and "look what it produced" is harder proof than "look, it replied."

Use realistic but clearly sample data — no real client names (the existing "Al Noor Trading" is an acceptable placeholder). Until a real export exists, build a faithful mockup of the document.
