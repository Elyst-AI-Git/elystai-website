## The differentiators (keep these — they're true and specific)
1. **Bilingual & local.** Taught in Malayalam + English, built for Indian and GCC professionals — not a generic Western course.
2. **Live, not just recorded.** Real sessions, real interaction — not a dumped video library.
3. **Community-backed.** Learning doesn't stop when the session ends; the Circle keeps you sharp.
4. **Taught by people who actually build AI.** The Services arm (AIOS) is live proof — practitioners shipping real AI products, not career educators reading slides.

Point 4 is the strongest and most unique — give it weight. It ties the two arms together: the people teaching you also build production AI for businesses.
